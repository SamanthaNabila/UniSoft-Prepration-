1. What problem does it solve?

UniVAT solves the problem of manually managing VAT calculation, documentation, reporting, and compliance. Businesses have many daily transactions, and manually calculating VAT or preparing VAT reports can lead to errors, delays, and compliance issues. UniVAT automates these processes and helps businesses manage their VAT-related activities more accurately and efficiently.

2. Who uses it every day?

UniVAT would mainly be used by finance and accounting teams, VAT/tax officers, business managers, and organizations that need to manage VAT compliance. It can also receive data from systems such as ERP, POS, accounting, banking, and e-commerce platforms, so employees working with those systems can be indirectly connected to UniVAT.

3. What data does it hold?

UniVAT handles VAT and business transaction-related data, such as:

Sales and purchase transactions
VAT amounts and VAT rates
Input tax and adjustments
Customer/supplier-related transaction information
VAT returns and reports
Mushak-related information
Compliance and filing records
Audit trails and transaction history

In simple words, it holds the information needed to calculate, track, report, and verify a company's VAT activities.

4. What would go wrong if it failed at 3 PM on a Thursday?
If UniVAT suddenly became unavailable during business hours, VAT-related operations could be disrupted. Employees might not be able to process or verify VAT transactions, generate required reports, or continue workflows that depend on UniVAT. If it is integrated with ERP, POS, accounting, or other systems, those connected workflows could also be affected.
